    Protection,

    An original modular armor concept, built around a stretch knit foundation, independent protective layers, and articulated outer plates.

    Rotate the mannequin, peel back the construction, or reveal how the pieces attach. The neck collar is concealed beneath the knit; the abdomen stays independent from the chest and waist.
